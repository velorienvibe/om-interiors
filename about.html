The Studio.

Located in Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh, Om Interiors is dedicated to elevating everyday spaces through precise, customized interior decoration.

Our Foundation

Customise everything related to interiors.

Based in the heart of Ramanayyapeta, Kakinada, Om Interiors serves as a comprehensive interior decorator for residential and commercial spaces. Our reputation is built on a commitment to quality, reflected in our 4.9-star rating across hundreds of verified reviews.

We do not believe in one-size-fits-all templates. Our approach is entirely bespoke, ensuring that every element of your space is thoughtfully considered, meticulously planned, and flawlessly executed.
